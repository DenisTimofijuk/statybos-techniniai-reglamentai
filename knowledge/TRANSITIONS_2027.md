# Transition on 2027-01-01

## STR 2.02.12:2026 „Pastatai“

Aplinkos ministro 2026-07-08 įsakymas Nr. D1-129 approves STR 2.02.12:2026 „Pastatai“.

**Effective date:** 2027-01-01.

The regulation sets essential design-solution requirements for buildings, related structures and their plots for new construction, reconstruction, repair, change of use, and formation/rearrangement of cadastral objects. For reconstruction/repair, the regulation states that its requirements apply to the altered part of the building.
