12. **Two adjacent private driveways at different elevations, radius markings and a retaining wall.** Legal/technical research route, checked 2026-11-02. A plan annotation such as `R=4 500` is a **horizontal curve radius** (4.5 m), not a surface elevation or a rule requiring one horizontal plane. A number `4,00` on a building's plot plan is a geometric dimension, not by itself proof that a separate four-metre-wide registered servitude has been granted to each neighbouring plot. Compare (a) the cadastral/servitude footprints, (b) the detail-plan binding designations, and (c) the approved vertical-design elevations **E** (existing) / **P** (projected). A building plan specifying elevations only for its owner's driveway **does not prescribe neighbouring driveway elevations**. Independent driveways may be designed at different heights, but any common traversable access, turning radius and shared junction must retain lawful, geometrically safe and continuous vehicle movement. The vertical split cannot remove the turning sweep, effective width or legal access promised by the servitude. Check STR 2.06.04:2014 current consolidated from **2026-06-02**, especially **46–51** for urban streets' vertical design and **81–82** for applicable street intersections; do **not** apply the 4% street-intersection requirement universally to private residential driveways without classification. Official e-Seimas current: https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/rs/actualedition/TAIS.413395/NHwVYDwISc/ . Other applicable requirements depend on road type, including STR 2.02.09:2005 9.6.1–9.6.2 through 2026-12-31 and new STR 2.02.12:2026 from 2027-01-01.
13. **Retaining wall boundary consent and servitude exception:** STR 1.05.01:2017 **appendix 7 points 2.1, 2.2 and 3** generally call for adjacent owner's written consent when installing a retaining wall directly on the boundary, or when a point's height exceeds its horizontal distance from the boundary, or when constructing other roofless engineering structures/roads within 1 m; **appendix 7 point 6 explicitly exempts the appendix's consent requirements where there are servitude-establishing documents for construction of engineering structures**. Crucial distinction: this exemption does *not* by itself extend the underlying servitude scope, authorize occupying land outside the authorized servitude, overrule safety/stability and runoff restrictions, or replace required design/SLD classification. A 1 m high retaining wall at or near a parcel boundary is **not automatically** permitted merely because a road servitude exists nearby. Check the precise location of footing, draining/backfill and any barrier against the registered servitude, including the required manoeuvring area.
