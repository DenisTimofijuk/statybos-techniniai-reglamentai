# Common decision paths

The same STR question usually spans several documents. Use these paths to avoid answering from the first keyword match.

## “Do I need a construction permit?”

1. **STR 1.01.08:2002** — classify the work: new construction, reconstruction, major/simple repair, demolition.
2. **STR 1.01.03:2017** — classify the structure/building and category where relevant.
3. **STR 1.05.01:2017** — determine whether an SLD is required for that work/category/location.
4. Check special-location triggers outside STR when relevant: cultural heritage, protected areas, special land-use conditions, etc.
5. Then check **STR 1.04.04:2017** for required project documentation.

## “Is this reconstruction or repair?”

Start with **STR 1.01.08:2002**. Do not decide from cost, visual appearance, or colloquial wording. The technical change to load-bearing structures / dimensions / engineering systems is what matters under the legal definitions.

Then follow the classification into:
- **STR 1.04.04:2017** for project requirements;
- **STR 1.05.01:2017** for permits/completion;
- **STR 1.06.01:2016** for execution/supervision.

## “What can I build on this plot / how close / how dense?”

Typical chain:
1. STR 1.01.03:2017 — intended structure/premises classification.
2. Applicable building-type STR (through 2026) or STR 2.02.12:2026 (from 2027-01-01).
3. STR 2.06.04:2014 for street/local-road interfaces where relevant.
4. STR 2.07.01:2003 for water/wastewater-network separation where relevant.
5. Planning documents, special land-use conditions and Civil Code neighbor rules may impose independent constraints. STR is rarely the entire answer.

## “What are the accessibility requirements?”

Primary: **STR 2.03.01:2019 „Statinių prieinamumas“**.

Also inspect:
- the applicable building-type regulation;
- entrances/routes, sanitary facilities, lifts and parking provisions;
- project scope under STR 1.04.04:2017;
- whether work is reconstruction/repair/change of use, because applicability can differ.

## “What structural loads / material design rules apply?”

Start with:
- **STR 2.05.03:2003** — structural design basis;
- **STR 2.05.04:2003** — actions and loads;
- material-specific STR: concrete (2.05.05), aluminium (2.05.06), timber (2.05.07), steel (2.05.08), masonry (2.05.09), etc.;
- **STR 2.05.21:2016** for geotechnical design.

Also check **STR 2.01.12:2024 „Statybų klimatologija“** for climatic parameters when the design calculation depends on them.

## “What must be in the project?”

Primary: **STR 1.04.04:2017 „Statinio projektavimas, projekto ekspertizė“**.

Before answering, determine:
- project type/stage;
- construction type;
- building category;
- whether expertise is mandatory;
- which project parts are applicable.

## “How is construction completed / registered?”

Primary: **STR 1.05.01:2017**.

Cross-check:
- STR 1.06.01:2016 for construction records and supervision;
- project/as-built requirements under STR 1.04.04:2017;
- cadastral-object rules under STR 1.07.03:2017 when formation/rearrangement is involved.

## “What ventilation/heating requirements apply?”

Primary: **STR 2.09.02:2005**.

Also check:
- energy performance: **STR 2.01.02:2016**;
- building envelope: **STR 2.04.01:2018**;
- use-specific building requirements (legacy 2.02.* through 2026; STR 2.02.12:2026 from 2027);
- hygiene/health requirements referenced by the applicable provisions.

## “Is this a simple/complex structure?”

Start with **STR 1.01.03:2017**, especially appendices defining special and non-complex categories and their dimensional/technical thresholds. Read table notes and exceptions, not only the numeric row.

## Date rule

A question about design/construction around **2027-01-01** must explicitly resolve whether the controlling date falls before or after the STR 2.02.12:2026 transition.
