# Common decision paths

The same STR question usually spans several documents. Use these paths to avoid answering from the first keyword match.

## “Do I need a construction permit?”

1. **STR 1.01.08:2002** — classify the work: new construction, reconstruction, major/simple repair, demolition.
2. **STR 1.01.03:2017** — classify the structure/building and category where relevant.
3. **STR 1.05.01:2017** — determine whether an SLD is required for that work/category/location.
4. Check special-location triggers outside STR when relevant: cultural heritage, protected areas, special land-use conditions, etc.
5. Then check **STR 1.04.04:2017** for required project documentation.

## “Is this reconstruction or repair?”

Start with **STR 1.01.08:2002**. Do not decide from cost, visual appearance, or colloquial wording. The technical change to load-bearing structures / dimensions / engineering systems is what matters under the legal definitions.

Then follow the classification into:
- **STR 1.04.04:2017** for project requirements;
- **STR 1.05.01:2017** for permits/completion;
- **STR 1.06.01:2016** for execution/supervision.

## “What can I build on this plot / how close / how dense?”

Typical chain:
1. STR 1.01.03:2017 — intended structure/premises classification.
2. Applicable building-type STR (through 2026) or STR 2.02.12:2026 (from 2027-01-01).
3. STR 2.06.04:2014 for street/local-road interfaces where relevant.
4. STR 2.07.01:2003 for water/wastewater-network separation where relevant.
5. Planning documents, special land-use conditions and Civil Code neighbor rules may impose independent constraints. STR is rarely the entire answer.

## “Can surface water be discharged to the neighbouring plot?”

For facts through **2026-12-31**, if the plot is governed by **STR 2.02.09:2005 „Vienbučiai ir dvibučiai gyvenamieji pastatai“**, start with **9.5.1–9.5.2**:
- where a communal/local storm sewer exists, surface runoff is to be led to that sewer;
- where it does not exist, runoff is to be led to ditches, channels, open water bodies, etc.;
- **9.5.2 expressly prohibits directing surface wastewater over the ground surface into neighbouring plots**.

Also check the general neighbour-protection rule in **Žemės įstatymo 21 straipsnio 7 punktas**: landowners/users conducting activity on their plot must not violate the rights and legally protected interests of neighbouring plot owners/users and residents.

Important distinction:
- deliberate/engineered redirection (grading, gutters, pipes, pumps, drains) is materially different from water following an unchanged natural terrain;
- if the neighbouring plot is not governed by the residential-building STR and its relief was altered independently of construction, route first to **Žemės įstatymo 21 straipsnio 7 punktas** rather than forcing the facts into STR 2.02.09:2005;
- VTPSI's 2021-09-30 published interpretation states that where relief forming is unrelated to construction/project documentation, the landowner must still comply with Žemės įstatymo 21(7), and the relief works must not violate neighbouring owners' rights;
- a 2025 Supreme Court case, **e3K-3-3-403/2025**, confirms the civil-law route under **CK 4.98** where relief/drainage conditions interfere with a neighbour's use of property; the remedy should require the infringement to be removed while allowing the responsible owner to choose a lawful technical solution;
- if old drainage ditches, collectors or subsurface drainage may exist, check **Melioracijos įstatymo 3, 5 and 6 straipsniai** and the melioration records before treating the issue as ordinary surface runoff;
- historical absorption or drainage across a neighbouring parcel should not be assumed, without further evidence, to create a registered/legal drainage right or servitude; check cadastral/servitude and melioration records;
- if the legal outcome depends on causation, inspect pre/post earthwork elevations, topographic surveys, drainage works, project vertical plans and flood evidence rather than relying only on who is currently lower.

## “How close to a neighbouring boundary may plants be planted?” (non-forest land)

**Research route; checked 2026-10-24.** This is not exclusively an STR question. Classify the plant (tree/shrub/liana vs herbaceous flower), land use (private residential/summer-garden vs agricultural crop vs forest), whether a hedge is being formed, side of plot, planted/formative height, date of planting, and neighbour's written consent.

1. **Aplinkos ministro 2007-12-29 įsakymas Nr. D1-717, „Želdynų įrengimo ir želdinių veisimo taisyklės“ (consolidated from 2022-01-20):** Rules 2, 5.1, 5.10, 5.11 and appendix rows 1–6. For trees and shrubs on private non-forest land without adjacent owner's/manager's written consent:
   - Individuals capable of exceeding 3 m, or formed above 3 m: **5 m north** (azimuth 315°–45° from centre of plot), **3 m** other sides.
   - Individuals capable of growing 2–3 m, or formed to 3 m: **2 m** all sides.
   - Individuals up to 2 m, or formed to 2 m: **1 m** all sides.
   - Hedge separating plots: **1 m**, maintained no higher than **1.3 m on north side**, **2 m other sides**.
   - Woody climbers/lianas: **0.5 m**; written consent required for growing them on boundary-adjoining walls/fences/installations (5.10).
   - The table distinguishes **height/formation**, *not merely tree versus shrub*. Never state all bushes can always be planted 1 m from the boundary. Written neighbour consent can allow a smaller appendix distance.
2. **STR 2.02.09:2005 „Vienbučiai ir dvibučiai gyvenamieji pastatai“, appendix 9, points 5.1–5.3 and 6** (applicable through 2026-12-31 in relevant residential plot setting): sets **at least** 1 m for shrubs/hedges, 2 m for trees up to 3 m, 3 m for other trees; north-side hedge max 1.3 m. Assess **alongside** the D1-717 appendix rather than assuming the STR's lower minimum overrides a stricter tree/shrub-height-specific requirement.
3. **Sodininkų bendrijų įstatymas (Nr. IX-1934), art. 6(8)** (current consolidated from 2024-01-01): in amateur garden plots, high trees at least 3 m (north 5 m absent neighbour consent), low trees 2 m, bushes 1 m; a hedge straddling the shared boundary needs written consent; point 8(4) allows narrower distances by written consent. Article 6(8) also explicitly refers plant care to Želdynų įstatymas and implementing regulations; cross-check D1-717 for taller bushes.
4. **Želdynų įstatymas (Nr. X-1241), art. 1(2) and 2** (consolidated from 2025-01-01): excludes **agricultural plants growing in agricultural-purpose land** and forest land (subject to its stated exceptions). Thus *fruit-bearing raspberries grown as agricultural crops* may have a different legal regime; do not automatically impose the ornamental-shrub distance without verifying land designation and purpose. Article 2 defines shrub through woody multi-stem growth, and “želdiniai” as trees, shrubs, lianas. **Herbaceous flowers (e.g., peonies) are not in the D1-717 tree/shrub setback categories**; no 1 m/2 m/3 m planting-distance requirement can be inferred merely because a flower is tall. Ownership/neighbour-right rules still apply.
5. For historical disputes verify the law **at planting date**. AAD's official planting guidance says that the D1-717 private-land boundary-distance requirement started **2016-08-25**, not retrospectively for earlier plantings; but older residential STR / amateur-garden / local rules may apply to earlier facts. Do not treat 2016 as blanket immunity for all earlier garden/residential plants.
6. From **2027-01-01** recheck any residential-plot cross-reference under **STR 2.02.12:2026 „Pastatai“**, which repeals STR 2.02.09:2005; D1-717 and other non-STR legislation must be checked independently.
7. If stems, branches or roots cross the boundary or genuinely interfere with use, check **Civil Code art. 4.42** (notice/opportunity to remove; no automatic self-help if they do not interfere) and **art. 4.98** where interference persists. Physical crossing and breach of planting distance are separate questions. Also check special-use/protection zones and any municipality-specific rules.

## “What are the accessibility requirements?”

Primary: **STR 2.03.01:2019 „Statinių prieinamumas“**.

Also inspect:
- the applicable building-type regulation;
- entrances/routes, sanitary facilities, lifts and parking provisions;
- project scope under STR 1.04.04:2017;
- whether work is reconstruction/repair/change of use, because applicability can differ.

## “What structural loads / material design rules apply?”

Start with:
- **STR 2.05.03:2003** — structural design basis;
- **STR 2.05.04:2003** — actions and loads;
- material-specific STR: concrete (2.05.05), aluminium (2.05.06), timber (2.05.07), steel (2.05.08), masonry (2.05.09), etc.;
- **STR 2.05.21:2016** for geotechnical design.

Also check **STR 2.01.12:2024 „Statybų klimatologija“** for climatic parameters when the design calculation depends on them.

## “What must be in the project?”

Primary: **STR 1.04.04:2017 „Statinio projektavimas, projekto ekspertizė“**.

Before answering, determine:
- project type/stage;
- construction type;
- building category;
- whether expertise is mandatory;
- which project parts are applicable.

## “How is construction completed / registered?”

Primary: **STR 1.05.01:2017**.

Cross-check:
- STR 1.06.01:2016 for construction records and supervision;
- project/as-built requirements under STR 1.04.04:2017;
- cadastral-object rules under STR 1.07.03:2017 when formation/rearrangement is involved.

## “What ventilation/heating requirements apply?”

Primary: **STR 2.09.02:2005**.

Also check:
- energy performance: **STR 2.01.02:2016**;
- building envelope: **STR 2.04.01:2018**;
- use-specific building requirements (legacy 2.02.* through 2026; STR 2.02.12:2026 from 2027);
- hygiene/health requirements referenced by the applicable provisions.

## “Is this a simple/complex structure?”

Start with **STR 1.01.03:2017**, especially appendices defining special and non-complex categories and their dimensional/technical thresholds. Read table notes and exceptions, not only the numeric row.

## Date rule

A question about design/construction around **2027-01-01** must explicitly resolve whether the controlling date falls before or after the STR 2.02.12:2026 transition.
